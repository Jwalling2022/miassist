MIassist — Software · 01

Professional inspections, right from your phone.

MIassist is a mobile inspection app built for field inspectors. Manage clients, conduct inspections, capture findings with photos, and generate professional PDF reports — all from your device.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What's inside

CLIENTS

Client & Facility Management

Organize your work by client and facility. Keep every inspection site in one place, ready when you are.

CHECKLISTS

Inspection Checklists

Use built-in templates — including STI SP001 — or customize checklists to match your own standards.

FINDINGS

Photo Findings

Capture findings with photos directly in the app. Document exactly what you see, right where you see it.

REPORTS

PDF Report Generation

Generate polished, professional PDF reports complete with your logo, signature, findings, and photos.

PROFILE

Inspector Profile & Signature

Set up your profile once — credentials, logo, and signature are applied automatically to every report.

SECURITY

Secure & Private

Your inspection data stays on your device. Sign in with Google or Apple for fast, secure account access.
